{\LARGE Supplementary Material}
\end{center}

This supplementary material lists implementation details used in the GrapeMaster platform. The main manuscript reports the platform architecture, data flow, analytical modules, validation design, and workflow verification; this document provides supplementary workflow figures, the software stack, selected data-model attributes, and backend-record screening rules for reproducibility and technical inspection.

\midrule

\toprule
Verification check & Operational criterion & Supported interpretation \\
\midrule
\endhead
Operational-account retention & An account is retained only when it has business records and field-crop-season linkage after deduplication and target-context screening & Defines the evaluated operational account set \\

Risk-to-operation linkage & Notification and task records are checked against retained user, field, or crop-season context & Shows whether risk or management events entered the operational workflow \\
Treatment-feedback linkage & Fungicide or pesticide records are checked through task identifiers and task completion status & Shows whether field operations can be converted into feedback records \\
Evidence-linkage availability & Image-analysis, advisory, disease-report, and note records are checked for available account, field, crop-season, image, or message links & Shows whether symptom evidence and advisory interactions are available for seasonal interpretation \\
Representative replay & A selected crop-season anchor must retrieve weather, phenology, risk, notification, task, and fungicide records through shared crop-season or task keys & Demonstrates reconstructability of one risk-to-feedback chain \\
Boundary reporting & Missing evidence under the replay anchor is explicitly reported rather than omitted & Separates verified linkage from unsupported production-outcome claims \\
Excluded outcome claim & Backend linkage and replay checks are not treated as evidence of pesticide reduction, disease-control efficacy, economic benefit, or user adoption & Maintains the distinction between platform-readiness evidence and agronomic-impact evidence \\
